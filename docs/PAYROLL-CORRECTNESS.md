# Payroll correctness and dated eligibility

Engineering implementation for review, 27 October 2026. Finance/pilot acceptance and deployment remain open. This branch is stacked on PR #201 for its dependency/Zod compatibility fixes; review the payroll diff against that branch until #201 is merged.

## Required setup

1. Apply the three additive payroll migrations and regenerate Prisma during the normal reviewed release process. Existing posted amounts stay unchanged. Existing `paidDays` remain unknown (null), rather than being invented as zero. Existing runs become REGULAR; one REGULAR per company/month remains enforced by a partial unique index. Off-cycle runs have independent request keys.
2. Configure active, postable 5100 EXPENSE (salary expense), 2300 LIABILITY (PPh 21 payable) and 2310 LIABILITY (BPJS payable). Check their accounting purpose as well as their codes before use. Set the default Bank / Cash Asset in Company Setup when more than one cash account exists. Setup refusal never chooses Inventory, AP or an arbitrary asset/expense/liability.
3. Save an effective-dated policy under Payroll settings: employee/employer BPJS rates, health wage floor/ceiling, JP ceiling, JKK risk tier, work-calendar holidays and the shortest workday for six-day schedules. Policies cover whole months, cannot overlap and cannot cross the next March JP update. Settings are explicitly confirmed company inputs, not an automatic statutory-rate feed. A saved policy is immutable; enter future non-overlapping policies. Correcting an erroneous saved policy needs a reviewed administrative correction workflow.
4. Each employee needs joining date, PTKP status and tax year, five/six-day schedule and fixed/variable allowance classification. Map other employee deductions to active company receivables/liabilities. Cash and all AR/AP controls are refused. BPJS and tax are calculated separately; do not duplicate them as manual deductions.
5. Use **End employment / employee leaving the company**, last working day (inclusive) and reason. Contract expiry is a separate review date: it does not silently terminate employment. Inactive legacy employees need a real last working day. Joining/final dates already used by posted payroll cannot be casually rewritten; use a separate record for a rehire.
6. Record all scheduled attendance. Approved ANNUAL, MATERNITY and PATERNITY leave are paid. Approved SICK requests count as paid sick days without a separate attendance record; raw SICK attendance still requires doctor certification. HALF_DAY counts 0.5; approved UNPAID is unpaid. OTHER uses an explicit paid/unpaid setting in Leave Management → Leave Types. Existing OTHER types remain unclassified until HR confirms them; the review message names classification, never missing attendance. Conflicting approved categories still block posting. Leave days/sick days stay separate from present days and are included in paidDays and the frozen calculation snapshot. Doctor certification is an authorized HR attestation checkbox; document upload is outside this change.
7. Before deployment/first payroll, open **Payroll employee setup pre-check** on the Payroll Run page and select the intended month/year. It lists every inactive employee without a last working day and every eligible employee needing PTKP/year confirmation, with edit links. Fix actual historical end dates before release; never invent dates or auto-copy PTKP into January. The read-only API is GET /api/v1/payroll-runs/precheck?month=1&year=2027 and is restricted to payroll viewers in the company.

## Calculation and posting

- Salary/fixed allowances are prorated using scheduled workdays in the selected month and inclusive employment dates. The supported calendar is Monday–Friday or Monday–Saturday minus configured holidays. Variable allowances are separately identified and currently paid in full even in a partial month: a Rp600k variable allowance stays Rp600k for a half-month joiner/leaver. Finance must review these cases and use explicit earning adjustments where appropriate; per-attendance allowance rules are a follow-up. BPJS wage basis is basic plus fixed allowances, using the period's confirmed rates/floors/ceilings.
- Monthly taxable gross includes cash earnings and employer JKK, JKM and BPJS Kesehatan. Employee JHT/JP are not subtracted from monthly TER. PTKP maps TK/0, TK/1, K/0 to A; TK/2, TK/3, K/1, K/2 to B; K/3 to C.
- December or the final employment month uses actual recorded year-to-date income, PTKP, 5% job expense (maximum Rp500,000 per employment month and Rp6,000,000/year), employee JHT/JP and Article 17 brackets. It subtracts previously posted withholding, allowing a refund. Missing prior salary months refuse reconciliation rather than assuming zero. PTKP must be consistent with earlier snapshots that year.
- Daily overtime uses 1/173 of basic plus fixed allowances, with the statutory 75% wage floor where applicable. Workdays use 1.5x then 2x; five/six-day rest/holiday bands and six-day shortest-holiday bands are applied per date. Daily limits and the 18-hour workday-overtime weekly limit are checked using the current month’s records only. A week spanning months is not fully checked; HR must review the adjacent month until cross-month validation is implemented. Monthly overtime totals are display summaries, not calculation inputs.
- REGULAR includes salary once and omits employees already paid salary in a posted run that month (including FINAL), without blocking remaining employees. If all employees were already paid, calculation refuses with no eligible lines. A stale REGULAR calculated before FINAL posts must be recalculated; duplicate REGULAR creation remains refused. THR uses explicit positive employee earnings. FINAL includes any remaining salary and reconciles tax. CORRECTION uses explicit signed earnings and a reason. These are ordinary salary/THR taxable adjustments, not an automatic severance-entitlement calculator. Combined monthly TER prevents each off-cycle run from starting a fresh tax bracket; BPJS is charged once for salary that month.
- Negative net pay needs an explicit employee recovery receivable. A zero-earning adjustment can supply that mapping for a fully unpaid salary month. Posting debits the receivable and does not invent a bank receipt. Positive employee net payments alone reduce cash.
- Other deductions credit their frozen account mappings. Employer BPJS increases expense; tax and BPJS use distinct payables. AR/AP controls are never document offsets. An account disabled after calculation also blocks approval.
- Company-scoped transaction locks serialize calculation, posting and approval. Snapshots store policy, employee dates/PTKP, daily overtime, paid/sick/leave day counts and approved leave classifications, adjustments, deduction accounts and prior posted run IDs. If another payroll posts, stale runs require recalculation. Pending approval and posted runs cannot be edited/recalculated/deleted; submission and approval repeat posting guards. Refused posting rolls back its financial/approval effects completely.

## Pilot blockers and operating workflow

**December 2026 / final pay is blocked for mid-year adopters without complete posted current-company YTD payroll.** There is currently no verified YTD opening entry screen or import. This review takes option (b): retain the refusal and record it as a pilot/release blocker, rather than implement a financial history-entry workflow in this patch. Do not fabricate attendance or regular runs to bypass it. A separate audited opening workflow needs verified tax gross, withholding, employee JHT/JP, months worked and coverage dates, duplicate/overlap protection and finance sign-off before this use case can be released (tracked in ROADMAP).

Attendance bulk entry currently handles one date at a time. For each scheduled workday, use Attendance → Entri Massal, select the date and only employees who actually worked, enter daily overtime if applicable and save. Repeat per date. Approved leave supplies payroll paid/unpaid days itself; leave those employees out of PRESENT entry. Skip holidays/rest days, keep HALF_DAY and certified SICK records accurate, and resolve missing-day messages before recalculation. Date-range bulk entry that skips leave/holidays is a follow-up; it is not implemented here.

Posting/approval still credits Bank/Cash immediately for positive net pay. A separate salary-payable approval and actual payment workflow is not implemented in this PR and is tracked in ROADMAP. Payment date on the current run remains its posting date.

## Verification and limits

Tests use a dedicated disposable PostgreSQL 16 container and newly created company data, not the running business database. `lib/__tests__/payroll-immediate.test.ts` covers each of the four original defects. `payroll-rules.test.ts` covers PTKP, annual tax, daily overtime and dated BPJS. `integration/payroll-correctness.int.test.ts` exercises real create/calculate/post/approval/edit routes with fixed GL values, monthly/final reconciliation, duplicate/concurrent runs, missing setup and complete refusal snapshots. `e2e/payroll-correctness.spec.ts` uses the employee, settings, attendance and payroll screens, including THR/correction and exact ledger checks.

Run normal unit tests, both TypeScript projects and builds. For database/browser acceptance, point DATABASE_URL at a disposable base database and use `test:int:setup` / `test:e2e:setup` before the corresponding suites. Those scripts derive `_test` / `_e2e` databases and rebuild them. Never point them at a business database.

Supported tax scope is ordinary resident monthly employees with complete current-company payroll history. This does not implement PPh 26, partial-year tax-residence annualization, prior-employer A1 imports, special severance tax, rotating shift calendars, holiday discovery, THR entitlement calculation or submission of tax/BPJS returns. Existing incomplete historical salary data requires verified history entry before final reconciliation; this change does not create a historical-import screen or rewrite past payroll. A company that started using the app after January cannot release December/final-pay payroll until verified YTD entry support exists. Finance must confirm policy values, unusual employment cases, leave entitlement and examples before release.
